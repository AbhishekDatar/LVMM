Our purpose

Read, write & speak Marathi

Las Vegas Marathi Shala is a non-profit school affiliated with Las Vegas Marathi Mandal. The purpose of Las Vegas Marathi Shala is to teach Marathi culture and Marathi language to the next generation in the Las Vegas area.

The aim of the Marathi Shala is to empower children with the ability to read, write and communicate in Marathi. Our next generation will have the opportunity to connect and communicate with their grandparents — primarily to preserve our legacy while we are away from Maharashtra.

Marathi language has been approved by CCSD for the Seal of Biliteracy on the high school diploma. For more information, contact Marathi Shala.

How the Shala works

Small batches, a flexible weekly schedule and occasional get-togethers in person.

Questions? Contact us at lvmmshala@gmail.com
