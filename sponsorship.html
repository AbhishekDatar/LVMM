Why your support matters

A young Mandal with big plans

As a new organization we will have expenses related to registration, conducting events for the Marathi community and having celebrity guests to perform.

  • RegistrationEstablishing and maintaining the Mandal as a registered not-for-profit organization.
  • Community eventsVenues, decorations, food and logistics for festivals like Ganeshotsav and Diwali.
  • Celebrity guest performancesBringing artists to Las Vegas to perform for our community.
