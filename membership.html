Questions

Membership FAQ

Who counts as a child member?

The Child membership ($20) is for children between 12 and 18 years of age. Anyone over 18 needs an Adult membership ($40).

Do children under 11 need a membership?

No — children under 11 years of age are free. Just include their names when you send us your member list.

I'm a college student. How do I join?

NSHE students join free! Email a photo of your NSHE ID and your phone number to lasvegasmarathimandal@gmail.com.

How do I send the names of all members?

Add a note at checkout listing everyone covered by your order, or email the names to lasvegasmarathimandal@gmail.com.

When does the early bird discount end?

The early bird discount on the Adult membership is available up to 31st January, 2026.
