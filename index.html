Our mission

Celebrating the culture, traditions & language of Maharashtra

Las Vegas Marathi Mandal is a not-for-profit organization founded to bring together the Marathi community in Las Vegas, Nevada — celebrating Marathi festivals and cultural events such as music, dance and theatre.

Festivals

Ganeshotsav, Diwali, Gudi Padwa and more — celebrated together, the Marathi way.
