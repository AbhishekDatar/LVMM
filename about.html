About LVMM

Our roots, our people, our desert home.

Las Vegas Marathi Mandal is a nonprofit cultural organization, desirous of preserving and promoting the rich cultural heritage of Maharashtra, India, and fostering cultural ties among the people of Marathi origin and culture.

Who we are

A little piece of Maharashtra in Las Vegas

Far from home, a shared language, a familiar festival or a favourite song can make a city feel like family. That is what LVMM is for.

We are a volunteer-run community of Marathi families, students and friends across Las Vegas and Henderson. Together we celebrate the festivals we grew up with, pass our language on to our children, and make space for music, dance, theatre and food from back home.

Our mission

Las Vegas Marathi Mandal is a not-for-profit organization and is founded with the goal of bringing together the Marathi community in Las Vegas, Nevada by celebrating Marathi festivals, cultural events such as music, dance and theatre, related to the culture, traditions and the language of the state of Maharashtra, India.
