Our mission

More than preserving — enriching

Vegas Yuva stands as a beacon for the Marathi youth of North America. An offshoot of Vegas Marathi Mandal, our primary mission is more than just preserving our rich Marathi culture.

It's about enriching the Marathi culture through the voices and actions of the next generation.

We believe in the power of community. By connecting Marathi youth across Vegas, we aim to foster lifelong friendships, build a platform for cultural exchange, and ensure the legacy of our ancestors thrives in the hearts of the young.
