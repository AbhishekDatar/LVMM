Our annual celebrations

Festivals we celebrate

Through the year the Mandal comes together for the festivals that mark the Marathi calendar. Dates are announced closer to each celebration.

Ganeshotsav

गणेशोत्सव

Welcoming Ganpati Bappa with aarti, dhol-tasha, dindi processions and modak.

Diwali

दीपोत्सव

Our biggest evening of the year — a cultural program, traditional faral and festive cheer.

Gudi Padwa

गुढीपाडवा

Ringing in the Marathi New Year by raising the gudi and starting afresh together.

Makar Sankranti & Haldi-Kunku

मकर संक्रांत · हळदी-कुंकू

तिळगुळ घ्या, गोड गोड बोला — sharing tilgul, sweetness and friendship.

Shiv Jayanti

शिवजयंती

Honouring Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj — a celebration our Vegas Yuva youth group helps bring to life.
